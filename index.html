What we improve

Three operational workstreams that protect the customer promise.

Each engagement starts with the problem in your operation, then builds the controls, ownership and reporting needed to manage it.

01 · Inventory control

Stock Accuracy & Inventory Visibility

Reduce the gap between what your systems say is available and what your team can actually pick, sell and fulfil.

Typical work

  • Reconcile physical, system and sales-channel stock
  • Set up cycle counts and variance investigation
  • Clarify adjustment approvals and low-stock alerts
  • Assign ownership for recurring discrepancies

What you receive

  • SKU / location variance register and root-cause view
  • Practical stock-control steps and review cadence
  • Manager-ready visibility of exceptions and value at risk
Example measuresInventory record accuracy · stock variance value · stock-out and oversell cancellations
02 · Fulfilment control

Order Exception Management

Give delayed, incomplete, misrouted or at-risk orders a visible route to resolution before they become avoidable customer frustration.

Typical work

  • Identify payment, picking, address, stock and courier exceptions
  • Build a prioritised queue with owner, due time and status
  • Define escalation and customer-update steps
  • Track repeat causes and ageing unresolved cases

What you receive

  • Exception categories and resolution workflow
  • Ownership and service-level matrix
  • Backlog view with reasons, age and next action
Example measuresExceptions per 1,000 orders · time to resolution · late / cancelled orders · repeat contacts
03 · Returns control

Returns & Refunds Case Management

Make every return traceable from request and inspection through to refund, replacement, exchange or stock recovery.

Typical work

  • Standardise return reasons, evidence and case statuses
  • Set inspection, disposition and refund decision steps
  • Define approval thresholds and customer communication
  • Identify items suitable for resale or supplier claims

What you receive

  • Returns reason-code and case workflow
  • Refund approval and escalation rules
  • Visibility of open cases, cycle time and recoverable value
Example measuresRefund cycle time · return rate by reason · value recovered · repeat contacts

Our method

From operational discovery to a measured improvement.

A staged engagement keeps the work practical, the scope clear and the decision to scale evidence-led.

1

Discovery

Understand your business model, operating teams, systems and the issue with the greatest impact.

You receive: agreed problem statement, stakeholders and discovery scope.
2

Operational Audit

Trace the current workflow, review available data and identify control gaps, recurring causes and exposure.

You receive: current-state map, baseline and prioritised findings.
3

Solution Design

Design the process, ownership, exception rules, reporting and any system changes needed.

You receive: future-state workflow, roles, measures and implementation plan.
4

Pilot

Test the agreed change with a defined team, product group, order flow or returns category.

You receive: working pilot, team guidance and issue log.
5

Measurement

Compare the pilot with the agreed baseline, review exceptions and document what changed.

You receive: KPI review, lessons and scale recommendation.
6

Scale & Optimise

Extend what works, refine the controls and establish an ongoing review rhythm if required.

You receive: rollout plan, operating ownership and improvement cadence.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Are you a software company?

No. ScaleNest is an e-commerce operations improvement and implementation partner. We help define and improve the process, ownership and reporting. Technology is used where it supports the agreed operating change.

Do we have to replace our current systems?

No. We begin with the tools and workflows you already use. If an integration or custom development is genuinely needed, we explain the gap and options before it becomes part of the scope.

What happens after the first conversation?

We clarify the operational problem and decide whether a defined discovery or audit is appropriate. You receive a proposed scope, deliverables, timing and fee before implementation work begins.

How do you measure success?

We agree a baseline and relevant KPIs before a pilot. Measures may include stock variance, oversell cancellations, exception resolution time, refund cycle time or recoverable value. Results are reported from the data available; we do not promise a number before understanding the operation.

What would our team need to provide?

Typically, access to the relevant process owners, a walkthrough of the current workflow and agreed samples of stock, order or returns data. Any system access and data handling are agreed with you before work starts.

Can you work alongside our existing team?

Yes. The aim is to make responsibilities and workflows clearer for the people doing the work, not to create a parallel operation. We involve the relevant retail, fulfilment and customer experience stakeholders throughout the engagement.
